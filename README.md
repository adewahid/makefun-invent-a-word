Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-invent-a-word-we-need-17a020e0) competition **Invent a Word We Need** — coin one new English word and define it in a single clear line of at most 140 characters.

## The entry

> **sendspasm — the jolt of spotting a typo the second after you hit send.**

70 characters, one line.

Why this word fills a real gap: every texter knows the stomach-drop of spotting a typo the instant a message leaves their hands, yet English has no single word for it — only long-winded descriptions. "sendspasm" names that exact jolt: "send" for the message already gone, "spasm" for the involuntary flinch that follows it. Built from two plain English parts, so anyone can say it, spell it, and remember it after one hearing.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`, enforcing the competition gate from `RULES.txt`: UTF-8 text of 1 to 140 characters, not only white space, a single line. It fails loudly if the entry falls outside the gate.

```sh
node main.ts
```
